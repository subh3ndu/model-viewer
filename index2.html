Explore the Sun Temple

Step into history at the Konark Sun Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Odisha, India. Crafted in the 13th century by King Narasimhadeva I, this architectural wonder takes the form of a massive chariot for Surya, the Sun God. Its 24 giant wheels and seven spirited horses carved from stone are a breathtaking sight.

As a foreign traveler, you’ll marvel at the temple’s ornate carvings—dancers, mythical creatures, and scenes of ancient life etched into every corner. Though time has weathered its walls, the temple’s elegance remains unmatched.

Visit at dawn or dusk to see the temple bathed in golden light, or time your trip for the Konark Dance Festival in December, where the stones come alive with music and movement.

Visitor Essentials

  • Location: Konark, Odisha, India
  • Entry Fee: $5 USD (foreigners)
  • Timings: 6:00 AM - 8:00 PM
  • Best Time: Oct - Mar
  • How to Reach: 35 km from Puri

Travel Tips

  • Wear comfy shoes for the stone paths.
  • Bring sunscreen—Odisha’s sun is fierce!
  • Opt for a guide to unlock hidden stories.
  • Photography’s welcome (no drones without permits).
